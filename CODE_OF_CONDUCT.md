# Code of Conduct

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration space for contributors of every
background, identity, experience level, and communication style.

Be specific about behavior and evidence. Critique code and decisions, not people. Respect
platform, accessibility, licensing, and privacy boundaries. Do not publish another person's
private information or use sexualized, threatening, insulting, or discriminatory language.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or participation that violate this
standard. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's profile contact
route. If no private route is available, open a brief public issue requesting private
contact without including sensitive details. Maintainers will review reports confidentially,
avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate the resulting participation decision.
